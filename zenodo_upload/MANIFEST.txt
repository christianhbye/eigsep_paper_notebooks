THE 2026-09-11 REGENERATION
---------------------------
Four files were rebuilt because the horizon model was corrected twice over:

  1. eigsep_terrain's dem.py read the float32 lidar GeoTIFFs as int32,
     truncating every elevation to a whole metre. Now float32.
  2. calc_horizon bins each DEM pixel into a whole number of azimuth bins and
     takes the max per bin, so at n_az=720 a horizontal antenna move re-sorts
     which pixel wins each bin and alpha_h jumps discontinuously. N_AZ is now
     46080 (0.0078 deg). Finer azimuth sampling resolves those flips more
     sharply but cannot remove them: below a degree the difference curves are
     still DEM pixel selection, not a response to the antenna moving. For East,
     corr(+d,-d) for a +/-0.1 m pair is -0.30 and the RMS ratio 1 m / 0.1 m is
     5.96 at native resolution, against a geometric -1 and 10; through a
     1.395 deg running mean they reach -0.998 and 10.06. Up passes either way.

     horizon_perturbations.npz therefore ships alpha_h at 46080 points,
     unsmoothed, plus smooth_deg = 360/258 deg. The simulations band-limit the
     curve to 720 bins before their sky mask point-samples it in azimuth -- one
     call to mock_analysis horizon_position/masks.py reduce_azimuth -- and
     Fig. 13 draws it through a smooth_deg running mean, the MWSS azimuth cell
     at which open_sky_weight samples the horizon. The mask's phi treatment is
     a known second-order approximation, measured at 0.60 per cent on dT_ant
     and tracked in eigsep_mock_analysis#10.

(2) was the substantive one; (1) barely moves the 720-bin result. Fig. 1 is
unchanged to every quoted digit (6/10/18 modes, 13.3/7.7/3.5 per cent retained,
N_ANCHOR 10). Fig. 14 keeps N_ANCHOR=10 and N_HAND=9; three quoted mK values
shift in the last digit and the caption's northward crossing goes 4 -> 2 modes,
because the northward dT_ant was overstated ~44 per cent by the binning
artefact. Fig. 13 draws the 46080-point curve through the 1.395 deg running
mean; its East cliff at az 170 peaks at 0.85 deg.

horizon_shift.npz's floors were ALSO stale for an unrelated, already-known
reason: the deposited copy carried the N=9 floors (nominal 1.82 mK, 30 mK at
10 m) while the manuscript quotes the corrected N=10 ones (0.62 mK, 3.4 mK) --
revision-round2-plan.md Step 0b. Both are fixed in this row.


WHY position_sims.npz IS NOW DEPOSITED
--------------------------------------
foreground_svd.npz is exactly row 0 of position_sims.npz plus metadata, and it
is all a reader needs to REDRAW the figures. It is not enough to CHECK the
analysis, and three numbers the paper quotes could not be verified from the
deposit at all:

  - max_dT_full = 9.5 K, quoted verbatim in the Fig. 14 caption, is the peak
    over all 1436 simulated LSTs. Recomputing it from the deposited dT_disp
    (24 LSTs) gives 8.9 K -- 6.3 per cent low. A referee checking that caption
    against the deposit would conclude it was wrong.
  - floor_own (the 0.53-0.73 mK range in section 4.7) requires an SVD of each
    DISPLACED waterfall. None were deposited.
  - cv_penalty, the held-out-LST ratio separating basis mismatch from
    in-sample optimism, needs the full LST set.

Deposited as float64 (40.9 MB; savez_compressed saves nothing on float64 sim
output). float32 would halve it to 19.3 MB at 3.1e-8 round-trip error, but
byte-equality is load-bearing here -- horizon_shift.ipynb asserts
np.array_equal(t_sys[0], foreground_svd t_sys) and
test_beam_comparison_beams_are_in_the_figure_order checks the bowtie row
against it -- so a precision seam would cost the property those checks rest on.
Precedent for the size: rfi_waterfall.npz is already 90 MB in the record.

Keep BOTH files. foreground_svd.npz is the 2.3 MB slice the notebooks name and
the tests reference; position_sims.npz is the full run behind it.
